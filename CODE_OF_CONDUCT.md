# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a technical collaboration space. Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence and behavior without personal attacks;
- respect different experience levels and hardware environments;
- give actionable, specific review feedback;
- avoid publishing another person's private information;
- disclose conflicts of interest and unverified claims;
- keep AI-generated material clearly attributable and reviewable.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and
unauthorized disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces managed by the repository owner.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through the maintainer account listed on the
repository profile or through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not include
sensitive evidence in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation
depending on severity and repetition. Reports will be handled with reasonable
confidentiality.
